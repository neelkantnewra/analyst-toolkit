Approval journey waterfall

Records move through the stages of the approval journey: Stage 1, then Stage 2, then Stage 3 and so on. A record that fires a rule at a stage drops out there. A record with no rule (blank or null) moves on to the next stage. Records that clear every stage are the final Passed bar.

1. Load data

Paste or upload either a pivot export (one column per stage: Stage 1, Stage 2, …, then a count column; subtotal rows and (null) cells are fine) or one row per record with a column per stage holding the rule(s) that fired, separated by | or ;. Blank or (null) = no rule fired.
